Detailed Description

template<typename BeliefType>
class storm::pomdp::beliefs::BeliefCollector< BeliefType >

Interns beliefs and assigns them consecutive identifiers.

IDs are stable for the collector's lifetime. References returned from the collector may be invalidated when new beliefs are inserted.
