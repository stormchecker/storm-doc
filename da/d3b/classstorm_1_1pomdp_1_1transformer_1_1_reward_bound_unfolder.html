Unfolds reward-bounded dimensions into the model state space and returns an equivalent formula.

See also
sections 4 and 5 of https://doi.org/10.18154/RWTH-2025-08686 for a formal definition of this transformation

Zero level widths unfold a dimension completely and remove its bound. Positive level widths retain a compact epoch representation and introduce a corresponding level reward model.
