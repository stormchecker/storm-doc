Detailed Description

template<typename RewardValueType, typename PomdpType, typename BeliefType>
class storm::pomdp::beliefs::RewardBoundedBeliefSplitter< RewardValueType, PomdpType, BeliefType >

Abstracts a given belief by splitting it into sub-beliefs based on the (state-action) reward of the POMDP.

Multiple reward models can be set. States in the support of the given belief where all action rewards are equal will be grouped together. When applying this as a pre-abstraction, this intuitively means that the collected rewards are observable.
