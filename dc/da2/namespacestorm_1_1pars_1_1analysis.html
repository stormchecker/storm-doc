◆ GraphPreservationStatus

enum class storm::pars::analysis::GraphPreservationStatus
strong

Describes to what extent a set of regions is known to be well-defined and to preserve the graph structure of the parametric model.

Enumerator
None 

No guarantee.

The graph structure of the model may change within a region. Only engines that can handle non-graph-preserving regions (currently robust parameter lifting) may be used in this case.

WellDefined 

The regions are known to be well-defined, i.e.

all probabilities lie in [0, 1] and all denominators are non-zero, but it is not known whether they preserve the graph structure of the model.

GraphPreserving 

The regions are known (or assumed) to preserve the graph structure of the model.

This implies that they are well-defined, as a model that is not well-formed cannot be graph-preserving.

Unknown 

It could not be established whether the regions are well-defined or preserve the graph structure of the model.
