template<typename ValueType>
struct storm::modelchecker::helper::DTMCSparseModelCheckingHelperReturnType< ValueType >

The outcome of a quantitative model checking query on a sparse DTMC.

Unlike its MDP counterpart it carries no scheduler, as a DTMC has no nondeterminism to resolve.
