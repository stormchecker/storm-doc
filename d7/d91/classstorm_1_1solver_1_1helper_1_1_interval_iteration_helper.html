Variant of interval iteration for floating point numbers with directed rounding.

The first vector of xy (and of offsets) holds the negated lower bounds, so that rounding upwards yields sound lower and upper bounds. It is up to the caller to set the rounding mode of the floating point environment to upwards and to negate the result again. If gamma is not zero, smooth updates are used (as in smoothII).

Smooth variant of interval iteration: Instead of taking over the bounds computed by the Bellman operator right away, the bounds only move towards the computed values.

The parameter gamma controls how far they move (the parameter delta is currently not used by the update).
