Detailed Description

Configuration for the SCP feasibility search (SparseScpInstantiationSearcher).

The trust region radius is a multiplicative factor >= 1: a variable may move to at most radius * v_hat and at least v_hat / radius, where v_hat is its value at the current linearization point. The radius shrinks towards 1 on rejected steps, so the search stops once radius - 1 falls below trustRegionMinExcess. A variable whose linearization value is exactly 0 has bounds [0, 0] and cannot move in later iterations.
