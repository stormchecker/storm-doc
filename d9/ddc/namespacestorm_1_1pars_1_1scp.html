The kind of property the LP is built for.

Probability states take values in [0,1] and may have a known value 1; reward states are unbounded above, have no known-value-1 case, and may have infinite value (see ScpLpEncoder's excludedRows parameter).

◆ ThresholdDirection

enum class storm::pars::scp::ThresholdDirection
strong

Which side of the threshold a witness is searched for, read robustly over schedulers:

AtLeast (threshold is Greater/GreaterEqual) <-> the minimal value (Pmin / Rmin) AtMost (threshold is Less/LessEqual) <-> the maximal value (Pmax / Rmax)

A Dtmc has no scheduler choice, but the same pairing selects the LP direction.
