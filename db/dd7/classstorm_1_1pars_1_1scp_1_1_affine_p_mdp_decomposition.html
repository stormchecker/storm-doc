Detailed Description

Decomposes the transition matrix of an affine pMDP (or pDTMC), and optionally one reward model's state-action rewards, into constant parts and (parameter, coefficient) pairs.

Every transition probability and reward must be affine in the parameters (total degree <= 1); otherwise construction throws. This makes the product P(s,alpha,s') * p_{s'} the only nonlinearity in the Bellman inequalities. The decomposition is purely structural and independent of the property's qualitative state sets.
