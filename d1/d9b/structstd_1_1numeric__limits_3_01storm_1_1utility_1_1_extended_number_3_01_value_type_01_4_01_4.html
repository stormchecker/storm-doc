Detailed Description

template<typename ValueType>
struct std::numeric_limits< storm::utility::ExtendedNumber< ValueType > >

Reports the infinity that ExtendedNumber adds, so that the generic storm::utility::infinity and storm::utility::isInfinity work on it without a special case.

The remaining traits are inherited from the underlying type where they still make sense.
