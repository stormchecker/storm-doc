Detailed Description

template<typename PomdpModelType, typename BeliefValueType = typename PomdpModelType::ValueType, typename BeliefMdpValueType = typename PomdpModelType::ValueType, typename PolicyValueType = BeliefMdpValueType>
class storm::pomdp::beliefs::BeliefBasedModelChecker< PomdpModelType, BeliefValueType, BeliefMdpValueType, PolicyValueType >

Builds and checks a finite belief MDP approximation of a POMDP.

The POMDP, belief, and generated MDP may use different value types. Callers provide preprocessing value bounds; these are used to value explicit frontier cut-offs when exploration is incomplete.

Explores the belief MDP by unfolding the belief space.

Exploration may stop early and use cut-offs or clipping.

Policy generation requires one continuation scheduler per cut-off bound: upper bounds for minimization, lower bounds for maximization.

Exceptions
storm::exceptions::IllegalArgumentExceptionif policy generation is requested with missing preprocessing bounds or a mismatched scheduler count.
Returns
the value, completion status, statistics, and optional policy produced by the checking run.
